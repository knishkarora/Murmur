# Murmur — AI Progress Companion

**Murmur** is an AI-powered conversational system that helps final-year engineering students and fresh graduates in India take one meaningful step forward each day.

It cuts through decision fatigue by focusing entirely on the **immediate next step**, translating tiny daily micro-actions into massive life and career direction.
